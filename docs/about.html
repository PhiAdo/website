About

My general research interests

  • Ibero-Romance pronominal syntax
  • Differential object marking
  • Second-language acquisition
  • Natural language processing
  • Forensic linguistics
  • Experimental and corpus linguistics
  • Syntax–semantics and syntax–discourse interfaces

Academic background

  • 2025–present: PhD in Romance Linguistics, University of Vienna. My dissertation examines direct object omission in Peninsular Spanish using quantitative methods.
  • 2018–2024: Staatsexamen Lehramt Gymnasium in Spanish and English, Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt.
  • 2018–2023: BA in Romance Linguistics, Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt.

Methods and tools

My research combines corpus compilation and annotation with acceptability, elicitation, and eye-tracking experiments. I use R, Python, and Stan for data analysis, as well as jsPsych and JATOS for experimental work.
