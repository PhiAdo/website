Event: Alpes 6 (Universität Zürich)

This talk focuses on the construction of a corpus specifically designed for the analysis of null objects in Spanish. Verb-driven Patterns of Object Omission in the History of Spanish: A Corpus-based Analysis

Date: 2025
Venue: Universitat de les Illes Balears (Palma, Spain)
Event: Null Objects from a Crosslinguistic and Developmental Perspective

This poster presentation gives insights into an ongoing diachronic corpus study of direct object omission in Spanish, based on a newly compiled and annotated corpus of texts from the 12th to the 18th century. It investigates whether verb classes and lexical semantics can predict patterns of null object realization, and evaluates these findings in relation to clitic doubling and existing diachronic accounts.

Un análisis de corpus sobre el marcado diferencial de objeto en hablantes de L2 español con L1 alemán

Date: 2025
Venue: Bergische Universität Wuppertal (Wuppertal, Germany)
Event: Invited Talk at Jornadas Lingüísticas BUW

This talk presents a corpus-based study of the acquisition of Differential Object Marking (DOM) in L2 Spanish by L1 German speakers, comparing learner and native usage. Using multifactorial random forest analyses, it examines the relative impact of semantic, pragmatic, and verbal factors on DOM realisation and omission.

Empirical Perspectives on the Object Agreement Cycle analysis of different varieties of Spanish

Date: 2025
Venue: University of Hamburg (Hamburg, Germany)
Event: Deutscher Hispanistentag: Sektion zum Status des Dativs

This talk investigates clitic doubling and null objects in Spanish from a diachronic corpus perspective, critically evaluating the Object Agreement Cycle. Drawing on historical data from multiple varieties, it shows that null objects precede and compete with clitic doubling, challenging strictly sequential accounts of object realisation.
